## Phase A — 導演預案

**English title:** *Stop Letting Overthinking Ruin Your Life*
**中文標題：**《別讓「想太多」毀了你》

**核心觀點：** 焦慮往往不是由現實造成，而是由尚未發生的失敗想像造成；打破內耗最有效的方法，是立即完成一個微小行動。

**Hook：** 事情還沒開始，大腦已經背上了一百種想像中的災難。

**畫面規格：** 16:9 橫式、黑色背景、白色火柴人。使用左—中—右橫向調度、側向跟拍、水平匹配剪輯和有意識的負空間。

**旁白：** 明亮、有力量的成年女性；自然美式英語；約 144 字；約 60 秒。

**強調色：**

- 高飽和焦慮紫：思想風暴和災難想像
- 鮮明危險紅：拒絕、牢籠和束縛
- 溫暖行動金：道路、能量和成長

**BGM：** 前半段為 72 BPM 的低沉循環鋼琴，加入時鐘、心跳和低頻；約 0:35 開始蓄力，0:40 切換到 108 BPM 的鼓點、弦樂和明亮合成器。

**情緒曲線：** 疲憊 → 恐懼升級 → 受困 → 認知反轉 → 爆發行動 → 釋放與成長。

## English VO transcript

Do you ever feel exhausted before you have even begun? Nothing has happened yet, but your mind is already carrying a hundred imaginary disasters.

Before the first step, you picture failure, judgment, rejection, and every possible way things could go wrong. Your thoughts turn possibilities into prisons.

Then come the questions: What if I fail? What will people think? The more you search for certainty, the tighter fear wraps around your feet.

But reality is rarely what hurts you most. It is the future your mind keeps inventing. Action does not require confidence; confidence follows action.

So interrupt the loop. Choose one tiny move and do it now. Send the message, open the document, take the first imperfect step.

The moment you move, fear loses power. Stop waiting to feel ready. Try, make mistakes, grow—and prove your thoughts do not control your life.

- Six approximately ten-second clips; 16:9 horizontal; 720p target; 24 FPS; synchronized audio.
- Pure black background with white stick figures and white line art.
- Circular head, no face, no hair, no clothing, stable proportions, uniform medium line weight.
- Saturated anxiety violet, vivid danger red, and warm action gold; use natural-language color names only.

- Generate no visible writing. Keep every card, bubble, clock, meter, notification, and interface element icon-only; add optional phrases in post-production.

### Stitching guide

1. Clip 1 violet tangle fills frame → Clip 2 travels through the identical tangle.
2. Clip 2 red prison bars extend down → Clip 3 transforms the same bars into vines.
3. Clip 3 gold beam spans the frame → Clip 4 bends the same beam into a road.
4. Clip 4 glass fragment fills the lens → Clip 5 moves the same fragment away to reveal A’s hands.
5. Clip 5 raised right foot pauses → Clip 6 lands the same foot from the same position.
6. Clip 6 holds the mountain, gold path, and clean overlay-safe negative space for approximately 12–18 frames; add the optional CTA in post-production.

Trim duplicate transition holds and use very short audio crossfades at each cut.
